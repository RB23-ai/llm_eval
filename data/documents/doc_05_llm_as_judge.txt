LLM-as-a-judge uses a separate model call to score the output of an
application, which is useful when correctness is subjective or too expensive
to check by hand at scale. A judge prompt typically includes the original
question, the generated answer, the scoring criteria, and sometimes a
reference answer to compare against. Because judges are themselves models,
they need to be validated against human graders before being trusted, often
by comparing judge scores to human scores on the same set of answers and
computing the mean absolute error between them. A judge that drifts far from
human judgment on a validation set should not be relied on for grading in
production, no matter how convenient it is to call.
