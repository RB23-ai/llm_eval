Offline evaluation runs a fixed golden dataset through the application before
it is deployed, which makes it useful for comparing prompts, models, or
retrieval settings under controlled and repeatable conditions. Online
evaluation instead watches real production traffic after deployment, tracking
signals like latency, cost, thumbs up or down feedback, escalation to a
human, and conversation abandonment. Online evaluation can catch problems that
never showed up in the golden dataset, since real users ask things engineers
did not anticipate. The two approaches are complementary: production failures
discovered online should be fed back into the offline golden dataset, closing
the loop so every new version is tested against the app's actual weaknesses.
